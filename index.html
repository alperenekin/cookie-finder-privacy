Privacy Policy

Cookie Finder for Yahoo Fantasy · Effective 22 October 2026

In short: the extension shows you two of your own Yahoo login values so you can copy them. It does not collect, store, send or share any data. Nothing leaves your browser unless you copy it yourself.

What the extension accesses

When you click the extension icon, it reads two cookies from your browser: T and Y. These are the values that keep you logged in to Yahoo. The extension reads them only while its popup is open, and only to display them to you.

How the values are used

  • The values are shown in the extension's popup.
  • They are copied to your clipboard only when you click a Copy button.
  • What you do with copied values, such as pasting them into an app, is up to you. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

What the extension does not do

  • It makes no network requests. It has no server and never sends your values anywhere.
  • It does not save your values. Nothing is kept after the popup closes.
  • It does not change or delete any cookies.
  • It does not read or change the content of web pages.
  • It has no analytics, tracking or ads, and needs no account.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

Permissions

  • Cookies: to read the T and Y cookies and show them to you.
  • Access to basketball.fantasysports.yahoo.com: Chrome only lets an extension read cookies for sites it has access to. This one Yahoo Fantasy site is the narrowest access that lets the extension read T and Y.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping your account safe

T and Y keep you logged in to your Yahoo account. Only paste them into apps you trust. Signing out of Yahoo ends that login session.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
